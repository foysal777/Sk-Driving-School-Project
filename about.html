Founded in 2010 by Grade-A Approved Driving Instructor Glenn Davidson, DriveMaster Academy was born out of a simple conviction: learning to drive should never be intimidating or stressful.

Over the last 16 years, we have grown from a single dual-control car into the region's highest-rated driving school, known for our warm, patient coaching methods, 98.4% first-time pass record, and unwavering commitment to road safety.

Our 4 Core Teaching Principles

We combine empathetic psychology with technical driving precision to make every lesson engaging, productive, and enjoyable.

Patience & Anxiety Relief

Every driver learns at their own pace. Our instructors are trained to eliminate nervousness, explain mistakes calmly, and build quiet confidence.
